Hardware Safety & Specs

  • Controller: ESP32-S3 (Xtensa Dual-Core LX7 @ 240 MHz)
  • Active Layout: 5 × 3 Modules (50 × 30 = 1,500 LEDs)
  • Data Output: GPIO 4 (via 74AHCT125 Level Shifter)
  • Power Supply: 5V / 100A (500W G-Power PSU)
  • Dynamic Power Cap: 25,000 mA (FastLED safety limiter)
  • mDNS Address: http://tinkerspace.local
  • AP Fallback IP: 192.168.4.1 (SSID: TinkerSpace-LED)

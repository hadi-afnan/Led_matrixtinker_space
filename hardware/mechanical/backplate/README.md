# Backplate Design & Structural Substrates

The backplate provides structural rigidity to the multi-module array and serves as the mounting plane for power distribution busbars, fuse blocks, and the controller enclosure.

---

## 📏 Dimensions by Layout

| Layout | Nominal Active Area | Recommended Backplate Size |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **5×3 Prototype** | 828.0 × 496.8 mm | **850.0 × 520.0 mm** (allows 11 mm perimeter bezel) |
| **4×3 Target** | 662.4 × 496.8 mm | **684.0 × 520.0 mm** (allows 11 mm perimeter bezel) |

---

## 🪵 Recommended Materials

1. **6 mm Clear or Smoked Acrylic (Laser Cut)**:
   - Rigid, lightweight, and professional aesthetic.
   - Allows rear wiring and fuse blocks to remain visible (maker exhibition style).
2. **2020 Aluminum T-Slot Extrusion Frame**:
   - Best for heavy wall installations.
   - 3D-printed corner brackets tie extrusions together; M4 T-nuts fasten module frames directly.
3. **6 mm CNC MDF or Plywood**:
   - Cost-effective classroom / makerspace alternative.

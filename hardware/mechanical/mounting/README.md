# Mounting Brackets & Enclosures

This directory covers wall brackets, desktop kickstands, and power supply mounting brackets for the display.

---

## 🔩 Mounting Configurations

### 1. French Cleat Wall Mount (Recommended for Large Displays)
- **Principle**: Two interlocking 45-degree angle wedges.
- **Top Cleat**: Screwed directly to wall studs.
- **Bottom Cleat**: Fastened to top rear extrusion/backplate of the display.
- **Advantage**: Allows the 5×3 display (~4.5 kg complete) to be easily hung and lifted off for servicing without tools.

### 2. Desktop Kickstand Feet
- 3D-printable dual support feet angled at 15 degrees backward.
- Fastens directly to bottom M3 frame bosses for table-top exhibitions.

### 3. G-Power PSU Mounting Bracket
- Heavy-duty printed PETG brackets holding the 5V/100A power supply securely against the rear frame with at least 25 mm clearance for cooling fan airflow.
